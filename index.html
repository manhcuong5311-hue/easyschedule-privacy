Privacy Policy

Easy Schedule (the iOS app and the booking website at easyschedule-ce98a.web.app) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Your Account

To use Easy Schedule you sign in with email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address (or the private relay address Apple provides) and a user ID. We also store the display name you choose and, if you add one, a small profile photo.

2. Events, Chats and Tasks

Events you create (title, date and time, color and icon, repeat settings, location if you add one, participants, their responses and time proposals) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they sync across your devices and with the people you share them with. A copy is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Messages you send in an event's chat and shared to-dos are stored with that event and are visible to its participants. Personal to-dos and notes stay on your device.

3. Sharing and Your Booking Page

Other people can book time with you through the app or your booking link. To prevent double bookings, your availability is readable by anyone who has your user ID or booking link: the start and end times of your busy periods (for events created in the app this can include the event title), your days off, your booking hours, your plan type, and your custom link name if you set one. Event details, notes, chats and participants are not part of your availability.

Partners you connect with can see your display name and profile photo. People who book you on the website sign in with Google; we save their display name (not their email) so you can see who booked.

4. Location (optional)

If you add a place to an event, we store the place name, address and coordinates with that event; participants can see it. When you tap "use current location", your device location is read once to fill in the place. To remind you when to leave, the app uses your current location on your device to ask Apple Maps for the travel time — this location is not uploaded to our servers. You can deny or change location access at any time in iOS Settings.

5. iPhone Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Busy times from iPhone Calendar", the app reads your other calendars on the device and uploads only the start and end times of busy periods to your availability — never titles, locations, notes or attendees. "Add to iPhone Calendar" writes an event to your device calendar only when you tap it. You can turn this off in the app or revoke Calendar access in iOS Settings.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can notify you about new events, messages, changes and reminders. Notifications are sent through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

7. Usage Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used (for example, that an event was created or a screen was opened) so we can improve it. This includes an app-instance identifier, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. It does not include the content of your events or messages. Crash reports may also be provided by Apple if you share them with developers.

8. Advertising

The free version of Easy Schedule shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Premium and Pro subscribers do not see ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (e.g., device model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., ad impressions and clicks)

Tracking permission: We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and you will see less personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: We use Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Rewarded ads: Free users can optionally watch a short ad to unlock one extra event for a day. Watching ads is never required.

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

9. Premium Subscription & Payments

All subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple's In-App Purchase system. Easy Schedule never receives your payment information, billing details or Apple ID credentials; we only learn which plan is active so we can unlock its features.

10. Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the providers needed to run the service: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics and Hosting), Google AdMob (ads in the free version) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase, Maps and push notifications). Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Events, their chats and shared to-dos are removed from our servers automatically shortly after the event has ended. A private copy of past events may remain on your device until you delete the app.
  • We keep simple counts linked to your account (for example, how many appointments you had at each hour of the day) until you delete your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings > Account). This permanently deletes your account, profile and photo, the events you own or created, your availability and booking link, and your sharing permissions.

12. Data We Do NOT Collect

Easy Schedule does not collect or request access to your contacts, photos library (other than a photo you pick for your profile) or health data.

13. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by Google Firebase with encryption at rest. Access rules ensure that only you, the participants of your events, and — for your availability only — people with your booking link can read your data.

14. Children's Privacy

Easy Schedule is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

15. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or delete your data (you can delete your account directly in the app)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Opt out of personalized ads (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in the EEA/UK: Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app)

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
