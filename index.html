Privacy Policy

Easy Schedule respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the rights you have regarding your data.

1. Event and Schedule Data

All calendar events created within the app are stored locally on your device or securely in Apple's iCloud if enabled. We do not collect, access, or share your event details with any third parties.

2. Shared Events

You may choose to share your schedule with others using a unique UID. This action is entirely optional and fully controlled by you. Shared data is only made accessible to users you explicitly grant permission to.

3. Advertising

The free version of Easy Schedule shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Premium and Pro subscribers do not see ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (e.g., device model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., ad impressions and clicks)

Tracking permission: We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared and you will see less personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: We use Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Rewarded ads: Free users can optionally watch a short ad to unlock one extra event for a day. Watching ads is never required.

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

Easy Schedule does not collect or request access to:

  • Contacts
  • Health data

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Premium Subscription & Payments

All subscriptions and payments are processed securely via Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Easy Schedule does not store or have access to your payment information, billing details, or Apple ID credentials.

6. Performance & Crash Data

We may collect anonymized crash reports and performance analytics through Apple’s system APIs (e.g., Apple Crash Logs). This data helps us improve app stability but does not contain personally identifiable information.

7. Data Retention

We retain only the minimal information required for app functionality. Event data remains on your device until you delete the app or remove it manually. We do not store any personal data on our servers.

8. Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard security practices, including encryption managed by Apple. We only share data with the service providers needed to run the app, such as Google Firebase (sign-in and sync) and, in the free version, Google AdMob (ads).

9. Children’s Privacy

Easy Schedule is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

10. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized ads (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in the EEA/UK: Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app)

If you want to exercise these rights, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
